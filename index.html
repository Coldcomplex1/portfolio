01 / 03

Forecasting, re-examined

Dự báo

Independent research · Sole author · May 2026 – present

ML & DL forecasting in the Vietnamese stock market

Paper accepted at VietResCon 2026. The expanded journal version is under revision after first-round peer review at Finance Research Open (Elsevier).

  • Screened 813 records down to 19 studies under PRISMA 2020, and appraised each one against a 9-domain validity rubric I designed.
  • Traced 90%+ “accuracy” claims to relabeled error or fit statistics, or to data leakage. None of the studies benchmarked against a naive forecast.
  • Re-ran the only public code-and-data artifact (PLOS ONE, 2025) and reproduced its results exactly. A zero-return forecast still beat it.
